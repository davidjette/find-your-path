Presenter notes

Game · Sheets

Bring

  • Laptop with the game open. It works with no internet once loaded.
  • One set of 4 sheets per team, plus spares.
  • Coins: 10 per team for Year 1, about 16 per team for Year 2. Pennies fit the spots.
  • Pencils. A real die and a real coin as backup.

Opening and my story 0-10

  • Slides 1-9, through "An entrepreneur is". Arrow keys or space bar.
  • Hands up: who has ever sold something? Take two or three answers.
  • Job title in two parts. Land the donuts joke.
  • Homium: first homes at a price families can afford.
  • The flop: solar panels and batteries, government program cancelled.
  • What changed: many sources of funding before a big program.
"Nobody gets it right the first time. Today you get to try it."

Set up the game 10-15

  • Kids sort themselves into Builders, Tellers and Protectors. Each team needs at least one of each, one sheet per role.
  • Pick a business, give it a name.
  • 10 coins, three sheets, one optional celebrity (1 coin on the face).
  • Do not explain scoring. If asked: "Real businesses do not get the rules either. Best guess."

Year 1 15-21

  • GO slide: press Start the clock (6 minutes).
  • While they work: type the company names on the Teams tab. Walk the room and collect them.
  • At 1 minute left: "Count the coins on each sheet and write the totals in the Year 1 box."

Year 1 reveal 21-29

  • Press "Coins down! See what happened".
  • Type each team's Build, Tell, Protect and celebrity. Teams can shout them out. The Coins column turns red if a team is over 10.
  • Three buttons, one at a time. Pause for reactions after each.
1. Who got customers? The smaller of Build and Tell. Ask a low-scoring team what happened.
2. Celebrity news. Superstar: customers double. Oops: lose 2. Read the headline out loud.
3. Risk Die. Roll bigger than Protect: lose the difference. Ask who is safe and why.
  • Then "Back to the show" for the recap slide.

Year 2 29-32

  • Hand out bonus coins: 1 per customer kept. The Year 2 tab shows each team's coin total in the Coins column.
  • Sheets get cleared. Start the 3-minute clock.
"What will your team do differently?"

Year 2 reveal and scoreboard 32-40

  • Same three buttons, faster. New celebrity news and a new die roll.
  • Scoreboard button: total customers over both years.

Debrief 40-50

  • What went wrong in Year 1?
  • What did your team change?
  • Who took a big risk? How did it go?
  • Tie back: my solar program got cancelled, and I changed my plan too.
"Try. Miss. Change it. Try again."

Rules cheat sheet

CustomersSmaller of Build and Tell. The celebrity coin counts in Tell.
SuperstarTeams that hired them: customers x2.
OopsTeams that hired them: minus 2 customers.
Risk DieRoll minus Protect is lost, if the roll is bigger. Never below 0.
Year 2 coins10 plus customers kept in Year 1.
Final scoreYear 1 kept plus Year 2 kept.

If something breaks

  • Laptop dies: Teams fill in the scorecard. Roll the real die. For celebrity news, pick one superstar and one oops yourself.
  • Wrong numbers typed: just fix the box. Scores update on their own.
  • Want different news or a new roll: "Start this reveal over" under the table.
  • Second class: press the second class button at the top. The first class stays saved. Rename either one on the Teams tab.
  • Short on time: skip the Year 2 celebrity news talk and go straight to the die.
  • Younger group: skip the blank lines and help with the Risk Die subtraction out loud.
